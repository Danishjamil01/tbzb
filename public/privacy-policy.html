Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary: The Bite Zone Bidar ("TBZB") respects your privacy. We only collect the necessary customer details (such as Name, Delivery Address, and Phone Number) required to fulfill food orders and deliver delicious meals to your doorstep in Bidar. We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

When you use The Bite Zone Bidar application or website, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Customer Name, Phone Number, and Delivery Address provided during checkout.
  • Order Information: Items selected, special cooking instructions, order value, and order history.
  • Camera & Gallery Access (Admin Only): Authorized kitchen admins may access the camera or photo gallery to capture or upload dish photos for menu management. Regular customers are never prompted for camera access.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for:

  • Preparing and delivering your food orders accurately in Bidar.
  • Sending WhatsApp order confirmations, delivery updates, and status receipts.
  • Providing customer support regarding food queries, allergies, or timing.
  • Improving kitchen menu availability and service quality.

3. Payment & UPI Transactions

TBZB utilizes direct UPI / QR code payment workflows and WhatsApp-facilitated order placement. We do not store or process credit/debit card numbers, bank passwords, or PINs on our servers.

4. Data Storage & Security

Customer orders and operational statuses are transmitted securely over HTTPS and stored using Google Firebase Firestore infrastructure with appropriate access controls. We take reasonable administrative and technical precautions to safeguard your information against unauthorized access.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or distribute your personal contact information to marketing agencies or third parties. Information is only shared with our local kitchen delivery partners to complete your physical food delivery.

6. Children's Privacy

Our food delivery service is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
